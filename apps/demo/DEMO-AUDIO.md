# Demo recordings

The demo serves Wikimedia Commons MP3 transcodes of three Scott Joplin recordings.

- [The Entertainer](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Entertainer_-_Scott_Joplin.ogg), performed and released into the public domain by Wikipedia contributor IE.
- [Maple Leaf Rag](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Maple_Leaf_Rag.ogg), excerpt recorded by Wikimedia Commons contributor Foil Fencer2 and marked public domain.
- [Pleasant Moments](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pleasant_Moments_Piano_Roll.ogg), Joplin's 1916 piano roll, digitized by Pianola.co.nz. Wikimedia Commons lists the recording as public domain.
